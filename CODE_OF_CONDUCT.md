# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want Vextra Studio to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone – whatever your age, experience, background, identity, ability or nationality. Beginners making their first game are as welcome as seasoned engine programmers.

## What we expect
- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good intentions.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Help newcomers; there are no "stupid questions".
- Stay on topic and keep issues and pull requests constructive.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and work (credit sources, respect licenses).

## What we do not accept
- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or hate speech of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Posting malware, exploits or other harmful content.

## Scope
This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, library content, and anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and temporarily or permanently ban people who break these rules. Reports are handled privately and fairly.

**To report a problem**, contact the maintainer, XHiddenProjects, through the repository's GitHub profile (private message) or the "Report content" option on GitHub. Security vulnerabilities go through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Consequences
1. **Reminder** – a private or public note explaining what was wrong.
2. **Warning** – a formal warning; further breaches have consequences.
3. **Temporary ban** – from interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban** – for serious or repeated breaches.
